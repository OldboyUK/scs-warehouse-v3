Please note, all files within the GOOGLE SCRIPTS folder are not hosted on Netlify. These scripts are connected to the Google Sheets back end and are deployed manually in Google Apps Script.

After deploying in Google Apps Script, set the web app /exec URL in Netlify environment variables:
- APPS_SCRIPT_URL (recommended — one URL for all functions), or
- Per-function keys: GOODS3P_SCRIPT_URL, PALLET_SCRIPT_URL, DISPATCH_SCRIPT_URL, LOCATION_SCRIPT_URL

Do not commit the /exec URL to this repository — Netlify secrets scanning will block the build.
